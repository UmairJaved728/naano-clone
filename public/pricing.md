# Naano Pricing

Last updated: 2026-09-12

Naano is the B2B LinkedIn creator marketplace. Brands book vetted creators for sponsored posts at fixed per-post prices set by the creators themselves.

## Plans

### Self-Serve — €0 / month
For teams running creator campaigns in-house.

- Creator marketplace access (3,000+ vetted B2B creators)
- AI-powered brief creation
- Track clicks, companies and pipeline (first-party attribution)
- Automatic creator payouts (Stripe Connect)

### Managed Campaigns — €700 / month
For teams that want Naano to operate the creator channel end to end.

- Campaign strategy and positioning
- Creator sourcing and coordination
- Brief creation and campaign launch
- Reporting and optimisation

Campaign spend is separate and priced per published post. No lock-in. Cancel anytime.

## Per-post creator pricing
- Prices are set by each creator and shown before booking.
- Typical range: €20 – €1,500 per published post.
- Brands pay only when a post goes live.
